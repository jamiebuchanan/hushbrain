## Decision

No peat based compost on the shelves from the 2026 season.

## Options considered

- Keep both ranges: ties up shelf space for the slower seller.
- Peat free only (chosen): simpler ordering, matches what customers ask for.

## Reversal cost

Low. A supplier can restart the range within two weeks.
